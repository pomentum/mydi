# TST-0004: A device that holds

**Target:** a device whose driver holds writes, or that comes and goes, troubles only its own port.
**Status:** Covered (Windows)

## The use
On stage a cable is pulled, a device is replugged, or a driver stalls. The other devices must keep their clock and their notes.

## How it is shown
- A port's writer held on purpose while another port keeps its clock; what waited released by its kind, note offs never dropped; a call
  that never returns given up (`ports_hold_test.go`, `write_delay_test.go`, `lander_test.go`) [V-exp: the suite].
- Devices coming and going counted, and until when Windows may hold writes for them (`devices_test.go`) [V-exp].
- On Windows, a device unplugged during a clock: the clock's count kept through a 215 ms hold
  ([the Windows report](../reports/midi-clock-on-windows.md)) [V-exp].

## Limits
A receiver still hears a hold the system makes; the rule is to plug every device in before the show ([the Windows guide](../guides/windows.md)).
