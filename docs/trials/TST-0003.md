# TST-0003: Measure a machine

**Target:** anyone measures their own machine's MIDI timing and where its system stamps input, with no extra hardware.
**Status:** Covered

## The use
Before trusting a machine with a clock, a musician or an integrator wants numbers: how late its pulses are, idle and loaded, as a
service, and whether its system stamps input on arrival. No logic analyser should be needed.

## How it is shown
- `mydi timing`, `mydi loopback` and `mydi follow` ([the measuring guide](../guides/measuring.md)), built in CI for Linux, arm64 and
  Windows.
- The loopback's analysis on generated stamps, with a negative control never held (`loopback_test.go`) [V-exp: the suite].
- The reports were made with the same measures ([docs/reports](../reports/README.md)) [V-exp].

## Limits
A measure through a device reads what the device sends back; what a device does with a pulse it does not echo is not measured.
