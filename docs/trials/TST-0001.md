# TST-0001: Play and clock a synth

**Target:** a program plays notes and clocks a synth from Go, on time on Windows and Linux, and leaves no note stuck when it goes.
**Status:** Covered

## The use
A sequencer, a generative patch or a show controller written in Go drives a hardware synth: notes with their times, controller moves,
and the MIDI clock the synth's own sequencer and effects follow. A note started must end, whatever happens to the program's parts.

## How it is shown
- Events encoded, parsed and played at their time; an owner's notes released and another's left alone; a port that fails
  (`midi_test.go`, `virtual_test.go`, `ports_test.go`) [V-exp: the suite, in CI].
- The clock on its grid: `clock_timing_test.go` (`MYDI_TIMING=1`) and `mydi timing --port <id>`; an hour through a synth on Windows,
  idle and loaded, as a service ([the Windows report](../reports/midi-clock-on-windows.md)) [V-exp]. A pass: the 99th percentile off the
  grid under 1 ms on a quiet machine, no pulse lost between Start and Stop.
- WinMM itself: `winmm_windows_test.go`, run on Windows.

## Limits
Linux's clock runs on Go's timers, not yet `clock_nanosleep` and `SCHED_FIFO` ([ADR-0003](../adrs/0003-one-time-thread.md) part E);
no hour-long run on a Raspberry Pi yet.
